# Verdigris voice

Verdigris is an invented English industrial borough in the late nineteenth
century. Its language should be legible now without sounding as though a product
manager, police procedural, or modern council website wrote it.

## Register

Use plain, concrete civic English. Prefer the words that would appear in a
minute book, inspector's return, local newspaper, or petition:

- Town Hall, council, committee, minute, bylaw, ratepayer, petition, deputation
- inspector of nuisances, premises, nuisance, return, notice to abate
- Works Register, works case, make good, execute the work, shelve the case
- public house, constabulary, lay an information, detention, retraction
- public purse, rates, arrears, pledge, sitting, chair

The Public Health Act 1875 uses the sequence of information received, nuisance
established, notice served upon an owner or occupier, and works executed. The
same Act uses "premises", "notice to abate", and a summons after default. That is
the model for sanitation and building copy, not modern enforcement vocabulary.

Municipal records of the period identify the town clerk, medical officer of
health, inspector of nuisances or sanitary inspector, and surveyor as distinct
officers. Verdigris can compress their work for play, but should use their names
accurately.

## Tone

- State the fact first. Dry irony may emerge from the fact, especially when
  paint replaces a repair, but the narrator should not keep nudging the player.
- Avoid modern abstractions such as stakeholder, credibility score, service
  delivery, action item, or working surface in flavour copy.
- Avoid fake antiquity. Do not add "whereas", "hereby", dialect spelling, or
  ornamental archaism unless a quoted legal notice requires it.
- Let numbers remain in clerk's notes and instrument readouts. Put natural speech
  in petitions, private words, and street reports.
- Do not use generic "someone", "somewhere", or "something" when the simulation
  has a name, address, firm, defect, or office available.
- Do not make the narrator sneer at every clerk, worker, or resident. Institutions
  may be captured or corrupt because stored state says so.
